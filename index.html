Rest you can hear

Sound healing uses the long, layered tones of singing bowls, gongs and chimes to slow everything down.

You lie comfortably, close your eyes and let the sound move around you. Most people notice their breathing soften within minutes, and many tell us it is the deepest rest they have had in a long time.

There is nothing to learn and nothing to get right. You simply listen.

Experience needed
None at all
What you do
Lie down, fully clothed

What happens in a session

Whether it is your first time or your fifteenth, every session follows the same unhurried rhythm.

  1. 1

    Arrive and settle

    Find your place on a mat with cushions, a blanket and an eye pillow. Arriving ten minutes early means you will not feel rushed.

  2. 2

    A short conversation

    We ask how you are feeling and whether there is anything on your mind. Share as much or as little as you like.

  3. 3

    The sound

    For around forty minutes, bowls, gongs and chimes are played around you. Some people drift off to sleep, and that is completely fine.

  4. 4

    Coming back

    The sound fades slowly. You rest a little longer, sit up in your own time and enjoy a warm cup of tea before you leave.

Questions

If you have a question that is not answered here, message us and we will be happy to help.

Do I need any experience?

None. If you can lie down and listen, you are ready. Many of the people we see have never tried meditation or sound healing before.

What should I wear and bring?

Wear comfortable clothes you can lie down in, and bring a water bottle. We provide mats, cushions, blankets and eye pillows.

Is sound healing suitable for everyone?

For most people, yes. If you are pregnant, have epilepsy, use a pacemaker or have a condition affected by sound, please tell us before booking so we can adjust the session or advise you. Sound healing supports wellbeing and is not a replacement for medical care.

How do I book?

Message us on WhatsApp, call, email or send the enquiry form below. We will reply with available times and confirm your booking personally.

Can I reschedule?

Yes. Let us know at least 24 hours before your session and we will find a new time that works for you.

Where do sessions take place?

At our studio, in your home, or at your workplace for team sessions. We will share the exact details when you book.
