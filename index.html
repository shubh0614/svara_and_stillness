Rest you can hear

Sound healing is an immersive journey of sound and guided meditation, gently inviting you into a space of stillness.

A space where you can slow down, breathe naturally, soften your thoughts, and gently release the tensions you may be carrying – both consciously and unconsciously.

What happens in a session

Every session is an invitation to slow down and turn inward – unfolding at an unhurried pace, whether it’s your first experience or your fifteenth.

  1. 01

    Arrive and settle

    Find your place on a mat with cushions, a blanket and an eye patch. Arriving ten minutes early means you will not feel rushed.

  2. 02

    Pause and connect

    Before the session begins, we take a few quiet moments to check in and create space for you to arrive fully.

    You may share how you’re feeling, what you’re carrying with you, or simply take a moment to settle in without saying a word. There is no right or wrong way to begin. We call this grounding – a gentle transition from the outside world into the stillness of the session.

  3. 03

    The sound

    The sound journey begins, creating a space for you to slow down, settle in, and turn inward.

    Simply lie back, close your eyes, breathe naturally, and allow the sounds to guide your experience. You may experience deep relaxation, moments of stillness, gentle awareness, or even drift into sleep.

    There is no particular way you need to feel or respond. Simply listen, surrender, and allow the experience to unfold at its own pace.

  4. 04

    Coming back

    As the sound journey comes to a close, we allow a few moments of silence for the experience to settle.

    You’ll be gently guided back to awareness, reconnecting with your breath, body, and surroundings. Take your time to move, stretch, and gradually sit up when you feel ready.

Questions

If you have a question that is not answered here, message us and we will be happy to help.

Do I need any experience?

None at all.

If you can lie down, get comfortable, and listen, you’re ready. No meditation or sound healing experience is needed – just come as you are.

What should I wear and bring?

Wear comfortable clothing that allows you to relax and lie down easily.

Is sound healing suitable for everyone?

Sound experiences are generally gentle and suitable for many people. However, if you are pregnant, have epilepsy, use a pacemaker, or have a condition that may be affected by sound or vibration, please let us know before booking.

We can discuss any adjustments that may be appropriate for your session.

Sound healing is a wellbeing experience and is not a substitute for medical care or treatment.

How do I book?

Message us on WhatsApp, call, email, or fill out the enquiry form below.

We’ll get back to you with available timings and personally confirm your session.

Can I reschedule?

Yes. Just let us know at least 24 hours before your session, and we’ll help you find another time that works for you.

Where do sessions take place?

Sessions can take place at our studio, in your home, or at your workplace for team sessions.

We’ll share the exact location and details when you book.
